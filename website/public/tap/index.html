Help with writing a LinkedIn profile link to your NFC sticker.

Copy the profile link

In LinkedIn, open the personal profile you want to share and copy its profile URL.

Write to one sticker

Return to Do Less Tap, paste the link and tap Write to sticker. Hold one writable NFC sticker still near the top of your iPhone.

Wait for Sticker ready

Keep the sticker in place while the app writes and reads the link back to check it.

Try your business card

Leave the app and hold the sticker near an unlocked NFC-capable phone. Tap the notification to open the profile.

Sticker help

Which stickers can I use?

Use a writable NFC sticker that supports NDEF link storage. A general RFID label may use a different technology and may not work with an iPhone. Stickers are purchased separately.

What if the sticker already has something on it?

The app asks before replacing existing contents. After you confirm, scan the same sticker again. A locked, read-only sticker cannot be rewritten.

My sticker is not being detected

Hold a single sticker near the top edge of your iPhone and keep it still. Try the sticker on its own, away from other stickers. If it still fails, check with the supplier that it supports writing an NDEF URL using an iPhone.

The app could not verify the write

The sticker may have moved before verification finished. Leave the app and try opening the sticker from your Home Screen. You can also write the same link again.

How do I change or remove the link?

Write a different profile link to replace the one on a writable sticker. To remove the stored contents completely, use an NFC utility that supports erasing tags. Do Less Tap currently writes LinkedIn personal profile links.

Does the other person need Do Less Tap?

Their phone only needs to read an NFC website link. Opening the profile may use LinkedIn or the phone's web browser, depending on the phone and its settings.

Need a hand?

Email hello@do-less.co with your iPhone model, iOS version and sticker type, plus the message you saw in the app. Keep private profile links or personal details out of your message unless they are needed to explain the problem.
