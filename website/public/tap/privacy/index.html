Privacy policy

How Do Less Tap handles your links, stickers and support questions.

Updated 18 November 2026

Who provides the app

Do Less Tap is provided by Samantha Raphael, trading as Do Less Co. You can contact us at hello@do-less.co.

Links and sticker contents

The profile link you enter and the sticker contents the app reads are processed on your iPhone. Do Less Tap does not send them to Do Less Co., store them on a server, or keep a saved history of your links or stickers.

Clipboard and NFC access

The app reads clipboard content when you choose Paste or paste into the link field. It starts an NFC scan when you choose Write to sticker or confirm a replacement. These functions are used to write and verify the link you selected.

What is saved on your sticker

The sticker stores the LinkedIn URL. Other people with a compatible NFC reader may read that URL. Treat the sticker as a public business card and write only a link you intend to share. The URL remains on the sticker until it is overwritten or erased using a compatible NFC tool.

Accounts, tracking and diagnostics

Do Less Tap does not require an account and includes no advertising, analytics or tracking software. Apple may process app diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple's own privacy policies.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use this to respond to your request and keep the correspondence only as long as needed to handle it and meet any legal obligations. Email us to request deletion of support correspondence.

External services

When a written link is opened, LinkedIn or a web browser handles it. Opening our website or sending a support email also uses the relevant website, email and hosting services. Those services process requests under their own privacy policies. Do Less Tap is independently made and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.

Changes and questions

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. The current policy is available in the app and on our website. For privacy questions, contact hello@do-less.co.
